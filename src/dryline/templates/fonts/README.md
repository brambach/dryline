Latin subsets from Fontsource (npm), self-hosted. All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org):

- Fraunces (Undercase Type), variable, all axes (opsz, wght, SOFT, WONK). dryline sets it soft and a
  little wonky, so it reads warm next to the engraving. `@fontsource-variable/fraunces@5.3.0`.
- Hanken Grotesk 400 and 500 (Alfredo Marco Pradil). `@fontsource/hanken-grotesk@5.3.0`.
- IBM Plex Mono 400 and 500 (IBM). `@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono@5.3.0`.

A serif speaks, a sans works, a mono labels (design-taste APPLY.md §1).
